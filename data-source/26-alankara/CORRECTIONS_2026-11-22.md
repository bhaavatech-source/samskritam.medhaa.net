# अलङ्कार-दत्तांशः — शोधनविवरणम् (Correction report)

Date: 2026-11-22
Input: `alankaras_complete-v2.json` (kept unchanged in this folder)
Output: `alankaras_corrected_2026-11-22.json`
Authority: Jayadeva, *Candrāloka*, Mayūkha 5, Subodh Chandra Pant edition (MLBD), the PDF supplied. The scan has no text layer. Verses were OCR'd (tesseract, Hindi model) and each verse used was then read from the page image. Book page = PDF page − 23.

## What was wrong (all 50 entries)

1. **Chandrāloka verse numbers were wrong in every entry.** Examples:
   - Anuprāsa, yamaka and vakrokti were cited as "चन्द्रालोकः २.३ / २.४ / २.५". Mayūkha 2 deals with doṣas; all three are in Mayūkha 5.
   - Most other entries cited two different 5.x numbers, and neither matched the book.
   - Every entry now cites 5.N with the page number of this edition.
2. **Lakṣaṇa verses were composites.** In most entries, the first line came from Kuvalayānanda, Kāvyaprakāśa or Sāhityadarpaṇa, or was half-remembered. The second line was invented: it appears in none of these works, and several such lines break the anuṣṭubh metre.
   - Every lakṣaṇa is now Jayadeva's own half-verse, as printed.
3. **Examples were wrong.** Many example lines were invented, or were Kuvalayānanda's versions presented as Candrāloka's. Some were wrong for the figure:
   - सार had उदारसार's example (5.91).
   - व्याघात's example has Manmatha burning Śambhu.
   - The Gaṅgālaharī attribution for व्याजस्तुति could not be traced.
4. **The meanings did not match the verses.** In several entries, the bhāva described a different verse altogether (ALK-046, 048, 049, 050 and others). All anvaya, bhāva (sa/en/te/hi) and samanvaya (sa/en/te/hi) were rewritten for the corrected verse.
5. **Kāvyaprakāśa and Sāhityadarpaṇa numbers could not be verified.** Numbers such as "काव्यप्रकाशः १०.४२२" do not follow either work's numbering and cannot be checked from the supplied sources, so they were removed.
   - Genuine classical examples are kept under `anya_udaharanani`, with the work named and verse numbers only where certain (Raghuvaṃśa 1.2, Śiśupālavadha 6.2).
   - Their readings were corrected: परागत, बद्धदृष्टिः, दर्भैः.
6. **Figures Jayadeva does not treat are marked.**
   - अल्पम् is a Kuvalayānanda addition. It is not in Candrāloka and not in this edition's contents list. Its verse could not be verified from the supplied sources.
   - सङ्कर and संसृष्टि: Jayadeva expressly denies that they are separate figures (5.119). Their examples are the ones the commentary itself gives (pp. 241–242).
7. **Category fixes.**
   - वक्रोक्ति is listed among arthālaṅkāras in Candrāloka (5.111); Mammaṭa treats it as a śabdālaṅkāra. Both are noted.
   - श्लेष follows Jayadeva's three kinds: खण्ड, भङ्ग, अर्थ (5.63–65).
8. **Types were replaced with Candrāloka's own sub-types**, with their verses, wherever Jayadeva gives them: अनुप्रास, उपमा, प्रतीप, रूपक, अपह्नुति, उत्प्रेक्षा, अतिशयोक्ति, सार. The standard pūrṇā/luptā/mālā types of upamā are kept and labelled as Kāvyaprakāśa/Sāhityadarpaṇa.
9. **Smaller fixes.**
   - Title typo: सम्पूणाऽलङ्कारमाला → सम्पूर्णालङ्कारमाला.
   - A few English names were adjusted (e.g. "Bimbi-Pratibimba" → "Bimba–Pratibimba").
   - `sloka_te` is regenerated by direct Devanagari→Telugu transliteration of the corrected verses.

## Checks run

- Every lakṣaṇa and example verse was metre-checked (16 syllables per anuṣṭubh half). All pass.
- The additional examples were also checked against their metres:
  - drutavilambita 12×4
  - śikhariṇī 17×4
  - sragdharā 21×4
- No Devanagari is left in the Telugu fields.
- Each entry's own corrections are listed in its `shodhana_tippani` field.

## Points for a reader to confirm against a second source

- ALK-034 अल्पम्: the Kuvalayānanda reading मणिमालोर्मिका (the input had मणिमूलोर्मिका).
- ALK-042: Pant prints उत्कण्ठितां च कलयन्. Other editions read उत्कण्ठिता च कुलटा, as the input had it.
- ALK-031: Pant prints मन्द नन्दी हरान्तिके.
